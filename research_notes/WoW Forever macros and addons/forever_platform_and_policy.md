# World of Warcraft: Forever — product, client lineage, and add-on/macro policy (state as of 12 November 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-12. Confidence tags used below: **[Official]** = Blizzard statement or Blizzard-authored artefact; **[Primary-code]** = read directly from the exported client UI source (Gethe/wow-ui-source `forever` branch); **[Wiki]** = warcraft.wiki.gg (community-maintained, usually reliable for API facts, but secondary); **[Press]** = news outlets; **[Low]** = guide sites, sales-driven blogs, or paraphrase of a video.

IMPORTANT CORRECTION TO THE BRIEF: the brief describes Forever as "reportedly in beta in late 2026". As of 12 November 2026 that is out of date. The beta ran 17 September to 21/22 October 2026 and the game **launched on 4 November 2026**. The live build read from the client UI source is **1.60.1.70888** (commit dated 10 November 2026). "16001" is not an arbitrary number: it is the TOC interface version for patch **1.60.1**.

## 1. What is World of Warcraft: Forever? Announcement, relationship to other versions, release state, beta access

### Takeaway
Forever is Blizzard's official "Classic+": a permanent, standalone, level-60 version of WoW built by the Classic team, announced at BlizzCon 2026 on 12 September 2026, in beta from 17 September to 21/22 October 2026, and live since 4 November 2026 on the normal WoW subscription. There is no longer a beta client to obtain; the beta was reached through pre-purchase bundles or an opt-in invitation via the Battle.net app.

Blizzard describes it as "a new beginning from the team behind WoW: Classic", positioned as a permanent home alongside modern WoW and Classic WoW. Level cap 60. Set after Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom and before Molten Core. New zones (Riverlands, Mount Hyjal), 9 new dungeons, two new max-level raids, revamped talent trees, account-based Legacy System, Campfire feature, a new race (Skyborne, High Elf exiles, either faction). — [Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/warcraft-forever-classic-announced-at-blizzcon-2026/)

### Inferences
- Forever is not Classic Era, not a Season of Discovery continuation, and not a retail expansion. It is a third, permanent product line with Classic-era content scope but (see section 2) the retail client and UI stack.
- Sources that say "beta ends 21 October" and "22 October" are both partly right: the stress test was on the 21st and the end-of-beta event on the 22nd. Sources that give 5 November for launch are reporting the same moment in a later time zone (23:00 UTC on 4 November).
- Any guide written during the beta (mid-September to late October) may describe behaviour that changed before launch; prefer sources dated on or after 4 November 2026.

### Gaps
- Blizzard's own announcement article and the official Forever site were not read directly; product description above is via Warcraft Tavern's reproduction and the wiki.
- No official statement was found on whether a Forever PTR or future public test client exists after launch.

## 2. Client build, interface (TOC) version, API lineage, flavour identifiers

### Takeaway
Forever is patch 1.60.1, TOC interface version 16001, and its UI and add-on API come from the modern retail ("Mainline") client at roughly patch 12.1.5, not from the Classic Era client. Internally it is the game type `camelot` (alias `forever` accepted in TOC files since beta build 70291), sitting beside `standard` (Midnight) in the Mainline code family.

### Cited Findings
- [Wiki] TOC interface version is **`16001`** for patch 1.60.1. Builds tracked: 70009 (23 Sep 2026), 70170 (1 Oct), 70291 (7 Oct; Beta 4 notes 8 Oct), 70425 (Beta 5, 16 Oct). — [Warcraft Wiki: Patch 1.60.1/API changes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.60.1/API_changes)
- [Primary-code, read 2026-11-12] The `forever` branch of the exported client UI source is tagged `1.60.1`; its `version.txt` reads **`1.60.1.70888`**; the commit is "1.60.1 (70888)" dated 10 November 2026. The repository also has separate branches `live` (tagged 12.1.5), `ptr` (tagged 12.1.7), `classic`, `classic_era`, `classic_anniversary`, `classic_titan`. — [Gethe/wow-ui-source, branch forever](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/tree/forever)
- [Official, 2026-09-15, via wiki summary] Blizzard post "An Update On Secrets In WoW: Forever": Forever shares Mainline's UI architecture and the vast majority of APIs available in 12.1.5. Forever and Midnight are two game types within the Mainline code family: **Camelot (to be renamed before launch)** and **Standard**. — [Warcraft Wiki: Patch 1.60.1/API changes/notes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.60.1/API_changes/notes); corroborated by [Gamewave (17 Sep 2026)](https://gamewave.fr/world-of-warcraft-forever/wow-forever-les-restrictions-d-addons-de-midnight-seront-aussi-appliquees/), which says the post was made in Blizzard's "WoW UI" Discord channel shortly before the beta opened
- [Wiki] The wiki's consolidated diff is "12.1.5 (69952) → 1.60.1 (70205)" dated 2 October 2026, i.e. Forever's API is documented as a delta from retail 12.1.5. Global API: 176 added, 10 removed. FrameXML: 471 added, 1041 removed. — [Warcraft Wiki: Patch 1.60.1/API changes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.60.1/API_changes)
- [Official, 2026-10-08, via wiki] Beta 4 (build 70291): "Forever" is accepted as an alias for Camelot in TOC suffixes (for example `MyAddon_Forever.toc`), in the `[Game]` TOC variable, and in game-type folder lookup. — [Warcraft Wiki: Patch 1.60.1/API changes/notes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.60.1/API_changes/notes)
- [Wiki] Identifiers documented for authors: TOC game type `forever` (equivalent to `camelot`); directives `AllowLoadGameType: forever` and `ExcludeLoadGameType: standard, classic`; metadata example `X-Game: Camelot`; Lua constant **`WOW_PROJECT_CAMELOT` = 18** (added 1 October 2026), checked as `WOW_PROJECT_ID == WOW_PROJECT_CAMELOT`; interface-number range check example `version > 16000 and version < 20000`. — [Warcraft Wiki: Patch 1.60.1/API changes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.60.1/API_changes)
- [Primary-code, build 70888] Blizzard's own UI still uses the name `camelot` after launch. Blizzard TOC files carry directives such as `## AllowLoadGameType: standard, camelot` (Blizzard_DamageMeter, Blizzard_CooldownViewer), `## AllowLoadGameType: classic, camelot` (Blizzard_GroupFinder_VanillaStyle), `## AllowLoadGameType: camelot` (Blizzard_LegacySystem), and per-file tags such as `[AllowLoadGameType camelot]`. — [Gethe/wow-ui-source, branch forever](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/tree/forever)
- [Primary-code, build 70888] Path counts in the `forever` tree by variant folder: `Mainline` 718, `Shared` 328, `Camelot` 212, `Classic` 93, `Cata` 29, `Vanilla` 26, `Mists` 10, `Standard` 8, `Wrath` 7, `TBC` 3. Forever-specific code is delivered as `Camelot/` override folders (for example `Blizzard_ActionBar/Camelot/ActionButtonOverrides.lua`, `Blizzard_EditMode/Camelot/...`, `Blizzard_FrameXML/Bindings_Camelot.xml` beside `Bindings_Standard.xml`). — [Gethe/wow-ui-source, branch forever](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/tree/forever)
- [Wiki] Ruleset detection uses `C_GameRules.IsGameRuleActive` with `Enum.GameRule.HardcoreRuleset`, `RPRuleset`, or `PvPRuleset`; otherwise PvE. — [Warcraft Wiki: Patch 1.60.1/API changes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.60.1/API_changes)
- [Wiki] Forever-specific API notes: `C_PvP.IsWarModeFeatureEnabled` returns false in Forever; `GetInspectHonorData` returns two extra values; entries marked new include `C_Spell.GetItemCooldown` and `UnitUsesAmmo`. — [Warcraft Wiki: Patch 1.60.1/API changes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.60.1/API_changes)
- [Press, 2026-09-17] Forever uses a version of the modern Midnight UI adjusted to Classic content. The article claims Forever loaded add-on and keybind settings from a previous WoW beta and advises disabling old beta add-ons to avoid Lua errors; two reader comments dispute the claim. — [Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/wow-forever-loads-your-retail-addons-keybinds/)
- [Low] "Classic-era addons won't load" and must be rebuilt against the Forever/Mainline API; Midnight add-ons should port more easily. — [Gfinity UI & Addons Setup Guide](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/world-of-warcraft-forever-ui-addons-setup-guide)

### Inferences
- The add-on API baseline for Forever is retail 12.1.5 minus retail-only systems plus a Forever layer. An author should start from a Midnight-compatible add-on, not a Classic Era one. Classic Era habits (unrestricted combat log parsing, reading unit auras and health freely in combat) do not transfer.
- The version number 1.60.1 places Forever in the "1.x" numbering space (Classic Era is 1.15.x), which is why the interface number 16001 is below 20000 even though the client is a 12.x-family build. Interface-number comparisons that assume "low number means old API" will misclassify Forever; use `WOW_PROJECT_ID` or the game type instead.
- Blizzard said `camelot` would be renamed before launch, but the launch-week build (70888) still uses `camelot` throughout Blizzard's own files, with `forever` only as an accepted alias. Authors should expect both names to remain valid, and should watch for a future rename.
- `Blizzard_GroupFinder_VanillaStyle` loading for both `classic` and `camelot` shows that some Classic-family UI modules were pulled into the Mainline tree for Forever; the lineage is "Mainline with selected Classic-style modules", not a merge of the two APIs.

### Gaps
- `WOW_PROJECT_CAMELOT = 18` is from the wiki only; I could not confirm the constant in the client source (the search of the lazily fetched repository timed out). Whether a `WOW_PROJECT_FOREVER` alias exists is unknown.
- The install directory name of the Forever client (the `_xxx_` folder under the WoW install) was not found in any source read.
- I did not read the full 260k-character wiki API-change page; the lists of the 176 added and 10 removed global functions are not itemised here.
- Whether the interface number has moved past 16001 since launch (for example a 1.60.2 patch) was not found; the latest build seen is 1.60.1.70888 (10 November 2026).

## 3. What Blizzard has officially said about add-ons and macros in Forever; the combat-information restrictions

### Takeaway
Blizzard confirmed on 15 September 2026 that Midnight's "addon disarmament", including the secret-values system, applies to Forever unchanged in kind: it is the same mechanism as retail 12.0, inherited through the shared Mainline client, not a separate Forever system. No Forever-specific official policy on macros was found.

### Cited Findings
- [Official, 2026-09-15, via wiki summary] "An Update On Secrets In WoW: Forever": the addon disarmament changes from Midnight, including secrets, will also apply in Forever. The AuraContainer/AuraButton changes from 12.1.0 will be available in Forever, and similar UI changes are expected in both Standard and Forever going forward. — [Warcraft Wiki: Patch 1.60.1/API changes/notes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.60.1/API_changes/notes); independently reported by [Gamewave (17 Sep 2026)](https://gamewave.fr/world-of-warcraft-forever/wow-forever-les-restrictions-d-addons-de-midnight-seront-aussi-appliquees/)
- [Press, search snippet only; page returned 403] Icy Veins reported the same statement: Forever "shares Mainline WoW's UI architecture, including the vast majority of APIs available in 12.1.5". — [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/addons-in-wow-forever-blizzard-devs-just-addressed-the-big-question/)
- [Press, headline only, 2026-09-16 per search summary] GamesRadar: Blizzard confirms access to add-ons will be restricted in Forever, taking "a page out of retail's book". — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/world-of-warcraft/world-of-warcraft-forever-takes-page-out-of-retails-book-as-blizzard-confirms-access-to-add-ons-will-be-restricted-in-the-throwback-mmo/)
- [Official, 2025-11-13] The governing design statement is Blizzard's Midnight post "Combat Philosophy and Addon Disarmament in Midnight", whose stated goal is that add-ons should no longer offer a competitive advantage in combat; it introduces secret values alongside new API hooks for authors. — [Blizzard News](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24246290/combat-philosophy-and-addon-disarmament-in-midnight)
- [Primary-code, build 70888] The Forever client ships the secret-values machinery: generated API documentation files `SecretAspectConstantsDocumentation.lua`, `SecretPredicateAPIDocumentation.lua`, `SecretPredicatesDocumentation.lua`, `SecretWrapperConstantsDocumentation.lua`; the `Blizzard_AuraContainer` add-on (with `Blizzard_AuraButton`, `Blizzard_CustomAuraContainer`, `UseSecureEnvironment: 1`, dependency on `Blizzard_PrivateAurasUI`); and `Blizzard_DeprecatedCombatLog`, `Blizzard_DeprecatedAuraFilters`, `Blizzard_DeprecatedInstanceEncounter` compatibility shims. — [Gethe/wow-ui-source, branch forever](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/tree/forever)
- [Official, 2026-10-08, via wiki] Forever beta 4 extended the secrets system: a new `FrameStrata` secret aspect was added, and `GetFrameStrata` returns a secret value for frames with that aspect; "a further issue allowing access to minimap rotation values during encounters was fixed". — [Warcraft Wiki: Patch 1.60.1/API changes/notes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.60.1/API_changes/notes)
- [Press interview, 2026-09-14] Ion Hazzikostas: "millions of players use add-ons today, and that has not changed"; Blizzard changed the API to give authors more direct access to some functions while restricting others used for gameplay advantage. The July 2026 "Curse of Ula'tek" retail update restored some functionality limited at Midnight's launch, including sound notifications used by accessibility add-ons; audio callouts are explicitly supported for accessibility. Holly Longdale on the Midnight rollout: "we could have communicated that better." — [Warcraft Tavern interview](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/ion-holly-interview-blizzcon-2026/)
- [Press interview, 2026-09-14] Ion Hazzikostas said Blizzard plans further changes to restrict interrupt trackers and is exploring default-UI ways to mark a primary interrupt target. — [Warcraft Tavern interview](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/ion-holly-interview-blizzcon-2026/)
- [Low, 2026-09-16; paraphrase of a MysticalOS (DBM author) video] Described rule of thumb: "displaying information is allowed. Telling the player what to do with it is not." Health, names, creature IDs, spell ID, exact spell name and target are described as secret in combat; spell-cast events signal that something happened but not what. The article characterises the Forever restrictions as "strictly identical" to retail's. — [Kami-Labs](https://kami-labs.fr/en/wow-classic/wow-forever-addons-restreints-comme-sur-midnight/)
- [Official, macros] The only Forever-specific official macro statements found are bug-fix and behaviour notes: (1 Oct) fixed macros on a gamepad action bar being unmovable; (8 Oct) fixed Specific Macros showing General Macros; (8 Oct) players can again swap multiple weapons within one global cooldown, including via macro. — [Blizzard forums: Development Notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2360696)
- [Official, 2026-10-08, via wiki] Chat-automation restriction relevant to macros and add-ons: `SendChatMessage` with type "EMOTE" now requires a hardware event outside instances, matching "SAY" and "YELL"; trial accounts can no longer send add-on messages. — [Warcraft Wiki: Patch 1.60.1/API changes/notes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.60.1/API_changes/notes)

### Inferences
- The brief's phrasing, "Forever hides buffs, debuffs and health from add-ons during combat", is directionally right but should be stated precisely: Forever uses Midnight's secret-values system, under which add-on code can receive and display certain combat values (through Blizzard widgets and secret-aware APIs) but cannot read, compare, or compute on them. It is the same system as retail 12.0, at its 12.1.x state including the 12.1.0 AuraContainer/AuraButton model. It is not a Forever-specific mechanism. The exact list of which values are secret in Forever, and under which conditions (combat, encounter, instance), was not verified from primary documentation in this research and should be taken from the secret-predicate documentation in the client or the wiki rather than from press summaries.
- Because Forever tracks the Mainline code family, future retail restriction changes (Blizzard said "similar UI changes are expected in both Standard and Forever") will likely arrive in Forever too, including the announced tightening on interrupt trackers.
- Macro conditionals that would branch on secret information are governed by the same Mainline rules as retail; nothing found suggests Forever relaxes or tightens macro rules relative to 12.1.5. This is an inference from shared architecture, not an official statement.

### Gaps
- The original text of the 15 September 2026 post was not read. It is in the WoW UI developer Discord (the wiki links it as discord.com/channels/327414731654692866/1549523107520188476), which is not publicly fetchable; all wording above is via the wiki summary and Gamewave's French paraphrase.
- No official, Forever-specific statement on macro policy (for example on spell-rank macros, downranking, cast sequences, or secure conditionals) was found.
- No Forever-specific official "why" statement was found beyond the Midnight philosophy post and the BlizzCon interview; the rationale is inherited from Midnight.
- I found no official list of what is secret in Forever that differs from retail; whether Classic-scope content (for example open-world, low-level dungeons) has different secrecy conditions is unverified.

## 4. Which add-on categories are affected or prohibited; built-in replacements

### Takeaway
No Forever-specific category list from Blizzard was found; the effective rule is Midnight's: add-ons that compute on combat state (WeakAuras-style conditional logic, rotation helpers, independent damage parsing, cast-based boss timers) are constrained, while presentation add-ons and non-combat tools continue. The Forever client ships Blizzard's Damage Meter, Cooldown Manager, AuraContainer, Edit Mode, and a Vanilla-style Group Finder, but the launch-week UI source does not load retail's Encounter Timeline or Encounter Warnings for Forever.

### Cited Findings
- [Primary-code, build 70888] Built-in modules that load for Forever: `Blizzard_DamageMeter` (`AllowLoadGameType: standard, camelot`, with a `Camelot\DamageMeterEntryOverrides.lua`), `Blizzard_CooldownViewer` (`standard, camelot`), `Blizzard_AuraContainer`, `Blizzard_PrivateAurasUI`, `Blizzard_EditMode` (with Camelot overrides), `Blizzard_NamePlates`, `Blizzard_GroupFinder_VanillaStyle` (`classic, camelot`), `Blizzard_AuctionHouseUI` (with a Camelot data file), `Blizzard_MacroUI`, gamepad modules, `Blizzard_LegacySystem` (`camelot` only). — [Gethe/wow-ui-source, branch forever](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/tree/forever)
- [Primary-code, build 70888] `Blizzard_EncounterTimeline` and `Blizzard_EncounterWarnings` are both marked `## AllowLoadGameType: standard`, i.e. present in the shared tree but not loaded in the Forever game type. — [Gethe/wow-ui-source, branch forever](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/tree/forever)

A "boss warnings" interface option was shown in the BlizzCon demo, with its final form in Forever unclear. — [Kami-Labs](https://kami-labs.fr/en/wow-classic/wow-forever-addons-restreints-comme-sur-midnight/)
- [Low, October 2026, via search summary; sales-oriented guide] Reported compatibility during beta: Questie, Details! and Deadly Boss Mods ship Forever files; WeakAuras has no official Forever build because its team stopped before Midnight; a community port named "Musca Auras" (described as a port of WeakAuras 5.22.0) ships a Forever build; statuses "change weekly". — [LFCarry guide](https://lfcarry.com/guides/wow-forever-addons)
- [Press] Named UI add-ons with Forever releases covered by news sites: ClassicUI Forever and EllesmereUI. — [Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/classicui-forever-addon-makes-wow-forever-truly-feel-like-classic/); [Icy Veins (not fetched; title only)](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/wow-forever-players-can-now-build-their-perfect-ui-with-ellesmereui/)
- [Low] Add-ons commonly recommended for Forever without verification of function: DBM/BigWigs, OmniCD, Plater, ElvUI, Leatrix Plus/ForeverPlus, Bagnon/AdiBags, Auctionator. — [Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/world-of-warcraft-forever-ui-addons-setup-guide)
- [Official, 2026-10-16, via wiki] Anti-layer-detection measure affecting utility add-ons: server ID and spawn ID in creature, vehicle, pet and game-object GUIDs are obfuscated (stable and unique within a session; GUID format otherwise unchanged). — [Warcraft Wiki: Patch 1.60.1/API changes/notes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.60.1/API_changes/notes)

### Inferences
- Category-by-category status for Forever (my synthesis; treat as inference, not Blizzard policy):
  - Damage meters: independent combat-log parsing is constrained as in Midnight; Blizzard's Damage Meter is present in Forever. Third-party meters work as reskins or consumers of Blizzard-provided data.
  - Boss mods: allowed in the restricted Midnight form. Notably, retail's built-in Encounter Timeline and Encounter Warnings are not enabled for Forever in build 70888, so Forever has less built-in encounter signalling than retail.
  - WeakAuras-style auras: display through AuraContainer/secret-aware widgets is possible; arbitrary conditional logic on secret values is not. The original WeakAuras project reportedly has no Forever build.
  - Unit frames and nameplates: allowed as presentation; values shown may be secret (displayable, not computable).
  - Rotation helpers: fall under the "telling the player what to do" class that Midnight's disarmament targets.
  - Interrupt trackers: currently possible in some form but explicitly flagged by Ion Hazzikostas for future restriction.
  - Auction/economy, quest, map, bag, and profession tools: no restriction reported; these do not depend on combat secrets.
  - Group-finder tools: nothing prohibiting them was found; Blizzard ships its own Vanilla-style Group Finder with voice options. Chat-driven LFG automation is limited by hardware-event requirements on SAY/YELL/EMOTE outside instances.
  - Layer-detection/layer-hopping helpers: deliberately broken by GUID obfuscation in Beta 5.
- "Prohibited" is the wrong frame for most of this: Blizzard enforces through API capability (secret values, hardware-event requirements, obfuscation), not through a published list of banned add-on types.

### Gaps
- No Blizzard-authored, Forever-specific list of permitted and prohibited add-on categories was found.
- The reason Encounter Timeline/Warnings are `standard`-only (deliberate design choice versus not yet ported) is not stated anywhere I could read. The BlizzCon demo reportedly showed a "boss warnings" option, which conflicts with the launch-week TOC directives; the feature may be delivered by another module or may be pending.
- Current (post-launch) compatibility of specific major add-ons (Details!, DBM, BigWigs, Plater, ElvUI, Auctionator, Questie, TSM) was not verified against their CurseForge/GitHub pages; the only compatibility data found is from low-confidence guide sites dated during the beta.
- The "Musca Auras" claim rests on a single sales-driven guide, seen only through a search summary; unverified.

## 6. Notable changes during the beta (and at launch) that add-on and macro authors must track

### Takeaway
Between 15 September and 10 November 2026 Blizzard shipped several author-facing changes: the `forever` TOC alias, a new secret aspect, tighter chat-automation rules, GUID obfuscation against layer detection, new nameplate CVars, reskinned unit frames, and per-input-type Edit Mode layouts. The internal name `camelot` is still pending a promised rename.

### Cited Findings
- [Official, 2026-09-15] Policy baseline set: Midnight restrictions and secrets apply; 12.1.0 AuraContainer/AuraButton model available; game type named Camelot "to be renamed before launch". — [Warcraft Wiki: notes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.60.1/API_changes/notes)
- [Wiki, 2026-10-01] `WOW_PROJECT_CAMELOT` (18) added. — [Warcraft Wiki: Patch 1.60.1/API changes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.60.1/API_changes)
- [Official, 2026-10-08, Beta 4, build 70291] — [Warcraft Wiki: notes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.60.1/API_changes/notes)
  - Trial accounts can no longer send add-on messages.
  - `SendChatMessage` "EMOTE" requires a hardware event outside instances (as SAY and YELL do).
  - Fixed a further issue allowing access to minimap rotation values during encounters.
  - New `FrameStrata` secret aspect; `GetFrameStrata` returns a secret value for frames with it.
  - "Forever" accepted as an alias for Camelot in TOC suffixes (`MyAddon_Forever.toc`), the `[Game]` variable, and game-type folder lookup.
  - The recently added `require` functionality was renamed to `import`.
  - `C_PvP.IsWarModeFeatureEnabled` returns false in Forever.
  - `GetInspectHonorData` returns two additional values.
  - Edit Mode layouts are saved separately per UI type (mouse and keyboard versus gamepad).
- [Official, 2026-10-16, Beta 5, build 70425] — [Warcraft Wiki: notes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.60.1/API_changes/notes)
  - Server ID and spawn ID in creature, vehicle, pet and game-object GUIDs are obfuscated to prevent layer detection.
  - Nameplates for non-target units far above or below the player are hidden, controlled by new CVars `nameplateMaxVerticalDistance` and `nameplateMaxVerticalDistanceAtMaxRange` (0 disables).
  - Importing an Edit Mode layout string from an older version no longer errors.
  - Forever player, target, pet, target-of-target, totem and focus frames and the minimap header were reskinned, with some child frames reorganised.
- [Official, 2026-10-08] Weapon swapping: multiple weapons can again be swapped within one global cooldown, including via macro. Macro UI fix for Specific versus General macros. — [Blizzard forums: Development Notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2360696)
- [Official, 2026-10-16] Ranked spells are a single Cooldown Manager entry. — [Blizzard forums: Development Notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2360696)
- [Official, 2026-11-03/04] Launch-build UI behaviour changes: newly learned spells are no longer auto-added to action bars; bags no longer auto-equip into bag slots; debuff icons on nameplates are click-through; nameplates now show Elite status; improved Gamepad UI compatibility with user-created add-ons. — [Blizzard forums: Development Notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2360696)
- [Primary-code, 2026-11-10] Latest exported build is 1.60.1.70888; Blizzard files still use `camelot`. — [Gethe/wow-ui-source, branch forever](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/tree/forever)
- [Press interview, 2026-09-14] Forward-looking: further restriction of interrupt trackers is planned. — [Warcraft Tavern interview](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/ion-holly-interview-blizzcon-2026/)
- [Low, 2026-09-16; MysticalOS prediction, not confirmed] Retail 12.2 may restrict unit spell-cast events further, with Forever following. — [Kami-Labs](https://kami-labs.fr/en/wow-classic/wow-forever-addons-restreints-comme-sur-midnight/)

### Inferences
- The reskin of unit frames with reorganised child frames (Beta 5) and the removal of auto-placement of new spells on action bars (launch) are the changes most likely to break add-ons and macro workflows written against early beta builds: frame-skinning add-ons that index child frames, and anything that assumed a newly trained spell rank appears on the bar automatically.
- The `require` to `import` rename indicates a new module-loading facility in the 12.1.x/1.60.1 add-on environment that was still being renamed in October 2026; code samples from before 8 October using `require` are stale.
- Authors should treat the game-type name as unstable: write TOC and Lua checks that tolerate both `camelot` and `forever`, and prefer `WOW_PROJECT_ID` checks.

### Gaps
- No API-facing notes were found for builds between 70425 (16 Oct) and 70888 (10 Nov); the wiki notes page as read ends at Beta 5. Whether launch or post-launch hotfixes changed add-on APIs is unknown.
- Hotfix notes dated 11 November 2026 exist (seen as a sidebar title on Warcraft Tavern) but were not read.
- Details of the `import` facility (semantics, sandboxing) were not researched; out of scope here but relevant to the code-focused researchers.
